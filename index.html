Researching stakeholders you haven't engaged yet
  1. Check the company's own leadership / about / team page first. It's the highest-confidence source for titles, scope, and location — this is how Rob Naso and Jonathan Epstein turned up for BGO.
  2. Search "[company] + [likely function]" (e.g. "asset management", "property technology", "innovation") to surface people who aren't on the leadership page, via LinkedIn, press releases, and aggregator sites (RocketReach, ZoomInfo, etc.).
  3. Use title hierarchy to infer a reporting line only when nothing states it directly — and mark it as inferred, not confirmed, in the Notes column.
  4. Check press releases and news for explicit "reporting to" language, and M&A / ownership announcements for parent-company ties (that's how the Sun Life / BGO ownership link was confirmed).
  5. Once your CRM/enrichment connectors (ZoomInfo, Apollo, HubSpot) are authorized, the same search-company then search-contacts pattern speeds this up for contact-level names beyond the leadership page.

Prefer to have Claude do this research first? Use the companion "Account Org Research Prompt" document — paste it into a chat, fill in the account name and what you already know, and Claude will hand back a pre-filled row set (Companies / Stakeholders / Relationships) ready to drop into this form.
